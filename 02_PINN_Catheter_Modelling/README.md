# Physics-informed catheter modelling

I worked on PINN-based catheter simulation and co-authored **A Data-Free PINN Framework for Static Modeling of Magnetically Steered Catheters** with Omid Shafiei, Hossein Nejat Pishkenari and Mahmoud Saadat Foumani.

The study builds static Cosserat equations and boundary conditions into neural-network training, then compares predicted catheter shapes with a numerical boundary-value solver. The supplied manuscript describes the method and results.

**Status:** accepted for ICBME; IEEE publication forthcoming, confirmed by me. No published DOI is claimed.

**What is supplied:** the accepted manuscript in `paper/` and this explanation. The final implementation is not on this laptop, confirmed by me on 5 November 2026. No earlier dynamic PINN notebook is substituted, and no paper-reproduction code or video is claimed.
